        NOTE: Category and Product view statistics are only kept for 30 days. All Dashboard Reports labeled "Top xxxx" (such as "Top Products") do not include Today's statistics, unless you specifically select the Date Range selection of "Today". Therefore it is recommended that you keep a few Dashboard Reports with "Today" as the Date Range, and then have a few more separate reports with any other Date Range you're interested in watching. This design provides the most optimized speed for report generation, while still providing you with a Real-Time view of all your business's analytics. However, keep in mind that all "Orders by Day " and "Orders By Month" reports do include up-to-the minute stats including today.
